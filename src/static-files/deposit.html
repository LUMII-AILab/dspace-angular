How to deposit

This guide describes a new submission to the CLARIN-LV repository. The sections, required fields and available options depend on the collection and your permissions. Follow the instructions shown in your submission form.

For an updated resource already in the repository, first see new versions and updating submitted data. The upstream CLARIN-DSpace new version guide provides additional background; available versioning actions depend on your permissions.

1. Log in

Only authenticated users can deposit items. Click Login at the top right of the page and use one of the sign-in methods offered. If you cannot sign in or need permission to submit, contact the Help Desk.

2. Start a new submission

After logging in, open Your submissions. Click the + button (New submission) to begin. If prompted, select the appropriate collection from those available to you. If you cannot start a submission or find the appropriate collection, contact the Help Desk.

3. Select the resource type and enter basic information

The form is organised into sections such as Describe, Upload files, Deposit license and Pick license, rather than separate pages with a Next button.

In Describe, select the type of resource you are submitting from the options offered, for example Corpus, and enter the basic information such as the title.

4. Describe your item

Describe the people, organisation and projects involved with the item. Add a description, language information and other details requested by the form. Complete the required fields before moving on to Upload files.

5. Upload files

Use browse in the upload area at the top of the form, or drag and drop your files there. Wait for the upload to finish, then check the files in Upload files. You can add more files and use the file controls to edit their details or remove a file before depositing.

If you are submitting a record without files, continue only if the collection permits this and the form does not require an upload. Ask the Help Desk if you are unsure.

6. Read the deposit agreement and choose a resource licence

The deposit agreement and the resource licence serve different purposes. In Deposit license, read the Distribution License Agreement linked from the form for your collection. If you agree, select Click to accept; the control changes to Accepted.

For uploaded resources, choose a licence that tells users how they may use the data or software. In Pick license, select the appropriate licence from the dropdown list. You can also consult the licence list.

If available, the OPEN License Selector can help you choose by asking questions about the resource and your requirements. Read the chosen licence before proceeding. If none of the licences suits your needs, save your work and contact the Help Desk.

Data requiring restricted access or licence signing

Before uploading such data, contact the Help Desk to confirm which access restrictions and licence-signing arrangements can be supported for your collection. Do not assume that selecting a licence alone sets up those restrictions. See also restricted submissions.

7. Leave a note, if offered

If the form includes a Note field for the reviewer, use it for any additional information about your submission. Its presence and position depend on the collection's form.

8. Review and save or deposit

Review the information, uploaded files and licence choices. Check the status icon beside each section heading: a check mark indicates a valid section; an exclamation mark indicates warnings or errors. Read the messages and correct any missing or invalid information.

Choose Save for later to save your work and continue later from Your submissions. When you are ready to submit, click Deposit and check the resulting confirmation or any errors.

9. After submission

If the collection requires editorial review, the item is sent to the editors and becomes available in the repository after approval. Editors may contact you for further details. Check the submission status in Your submissions and contact the Help Desk if you need assistance.
